THE PALACE RULE

Everything here is fantasy. Nothing here is authority.

IRC, character pages, blog links, dialogue, and visual-novel scenes cannot approve or execute system, PBX, network, account, filesystem, container, reset, or administrative actions. ZealPalace never receives private SEELE or PKM context, credentials, personal file contents, or raw call transcripts.

Sanitized read-only facts—latency, weather, device presence, PBX activity, and service health—may drift through as packet rain or airship weather. They are scenery only.
